  • 100.
    (a) Sample: "Two numbers add to 12, and one is 4 more than the other.
    What are they?" → x + y = 12 and y = x + 4. Substitute:
    x + (x + 4) = 12 → 2x + 4 = 12 → 2x = 8 → x = 4, y = 8.
    Check: 4 + 8 = 12 ✅ and 8 = 4 + 4 ✅.
    (b) Sample: "I multiply my number by 2, add 4, divide by 2, subtract
    7, and get 5." Undo backwards: 5 + 7 = 12 (undo −7), 12 × 2 = 24
    (undo ÷2), 24 − 4 = 20 (undo +4), 20 ÷ 2 = 10 (undo ×2). Forward
    check: 10 × 2 = 20, +4 = 24, ÷2 = 12, −7 = 5 ✅. Your puzzle may
    differ — undo it cleanly and it works.
