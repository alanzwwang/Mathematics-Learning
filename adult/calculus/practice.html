Problem Set

60 problems

Overview1234567891011121314ProblemsAssessment

The sixty problems below are graded in three groups. The Core problems check the definitions and standard computations of each lesson. The Advanced problems require a short proof or a multi-step calculation. The Challenge problems are proofs of theorems that were only stated or sketched in the lessons, together with some classical computations; each could be an exercise in a first-year graduate course.

Core (Problems 1–20)

  • 1.
    Find sup and inf of A = {n/(n + 1) : n ∈ ℕ} and of B = {x ∈ ℚ : x² < 3}, the latter computed in ℝ. Which are attained?
  • 2.
    Prove from the definition that aₙ = (2n² + 1)/(n² + 3) converges to 2, and find the least N with |aₙ − 2| < 0.001 for all n ≥ N.
  • 3.
    Let a₁ = 0 and aₙ₊₁ = √(12 + aₙ). Prove that (aₙ) converges and find its limit.
  • 4.
    For aₙ = (1 + (−1)ⁿ)/2 + 1/n, find sup aₙ, inf aₙ, lim sup aₙ and lim inf aₙ.
  • 5.
    Does ∑ n/(n³ + 1) converge? Justify.
  • 6.
    Show that ∑ₙ₌₀∞ 3ⁿ/n! converges and find its sum to four decimal places.
  • 7.
    Find the sum of ∑ₙ₌₁∞ 1/(n(n + 2)).
  • 8.
    Find the interval of convergence of ∑ₙ₌₁∞ (2ⁿ/n) xⁿ, including the behaviour at the endpoints.
  • 9.
    Find an explicit δ(ε) proving lim (x → 2) 1/x = 1/2.
  • 10.
    Prove that f(x) = 1/x is uniformly continuous on [1, ∞) but not on (0, 1].
  • 11.
    Find the number c given by the mean value theorem for f(x) = x² + 2x on [1, 4].
  • 12.
    Compute lim (x → 0) (1 − cos x)/x² and lim (x → 0⁺) x ln x.
  • 13.
    Find the Maclaurin polynomial of degree 4 of eˣ cos x.
  • 14.
    For f(x) = x² on [0, 1] with the uniform partition into n parts, U − L = 1/n. Find the least n with U − L < 10⁻³.
  • 15.
    Compute d/dx ∫ from sin x to 1 of √(1 + t²) dt.
  • 16.
    Compute ∫₀¹ x eˣ dx.
  • 17.
    Evaluate the improper integrals ∫₁∞ x⁻³ dx and ∫₀¹ x−1/3 dx.
  • 18.
    Does fₙ(x) = xⁿ converge uniformly on [0, 1/2]? On [0, 1)?
  • 19.
    Find the Jacobian matrix and Jacobian determinant of f(x, y) = (x²y, x + y³) at (1, 2).
  • 20.
    Compute ∫∫ over the disc x² + y² ≤ 4 of √(x² + y²) dA.

Advanced (Problems 21–45)

  • 21.
    Using only the least upper bound property, prove that every nonempty subset of ℝ that is bounded below has an infimum.
  • 22.
    Prove that every Cauchy sequence is bounded, and that a Cauchy sequence with a convergent subsequence converges (in any metric space).
  • 23.
    Decide the convergence of ∑ n!/nⁿ and of ∑ nⁿ/n!.
  • 24.
    Decide the convergence of ∑ (n/(n + 1))n².
  • 25.
    For which p > 0 does ∑ₙ₌₂∞ 1/(n (ln n)ᵖ) converge?
  • 26.
    Find the sum of the rearrangement 1 + 1/3 − 1/2 + 1/5 + 1/7 − 1/4 + 1/9 + 1/11 − 1/6 + ... of the alternating harmonic series (two positive terms, then one negative).
  • 27.
    Prove that |sin 1 + sin 2 + ... + sin n| ≤ 1/sin(1/2) for all n, and deduce that ∑ sin(n)/n converges.
  • 28.
    Prove that Thomae's function is continuous at every irrational and discontinuous at every rational.
  • 29.
    Prove that √x is uniformly continuous on [0, ∞) but not Lipschitz on [0, 1].
  • 30.
    Let f(x) = x² sin(1/x) and g(x) = x sin(1/x) for x ≠ 0, with f(0) = g(0) = 0. Show f′(0) exists but f′ is not continuous at 0, and that g′(0) does not exist.
  • 31.
    Prove that there is no function f on [−1, 1] with f′(x) = −1 for x < 0 and f′(x) = 1 for x ≥ 0.
  • 32.
    Prove that eˣ > 1 + x + x²/2 for x > 0 and eˣ < 1 + x + x²/2 for x < 0.
  • 33.
    Approximate ln(1.2) by its degree-3 Taylor polynomial at 0, and bound the error using the Lagrange remainder.
  • 34.
    Compute lim (x → 0) (tan x − x)/x³ and lim (x → 0) (1/sin²x − 1/x²).
  • 35.
    For f(x) = √x on [0, 4] with the uniform partition into n parts, compute U − L, and find the least n with U − L < 0.01.
  • 36.
    Prove that ∫₀∞ (sin x)/x dx converges but ∫₀∞ |sin x|/x dx diverges.
  • 37.
    Compute Γ(7/2) and ∫₀∞ x⁴e−x² dx in terms of √π, and give decimal values.
  • 38.
    Compute lim (n → ∞) ∫₀¹ nx(1 − x²)ⁿ dx, find the pointwise limit of the integrands, and explain why the convergence is not uniform.
  • 39.
    Let fₙ(x) = x/(1 + nx²). Show fₙ → 0 uniformly on ℝ but fₙ′(0) does not converge to the derivative of the limit.
  • 40.
    Find the radius of convergence of ∑ ((n!)²/(2n)!) xⁿ.
  • 41.
    Derive a closed form for ∑ₙ₌₁∞ n³xⁿ on (−1, 1) and evaluate ∑ n³/2ⁿ.
  • 42.
    Show that ∫₀¹ dx/(1 + x³) = ∑ₙ₌₀∞ (−1)ⁿ/(3n + 1), justifying the interchange, and evaluate both to four decimal places using (1/3)ln 2 + π/(3√3).
  • 43.
    Let f(x, y) = x²y/(x⁴ + y²) with f(0, 0) = 0. Show that every directional derivative of f at the origin exists but f is not continuous there.
  • 44.
    The folium of Descartes x³ + y³ = 6xy passes through (3, 3). Show y is locally a C¹ function of x there and compute dy/dx.
  • 45.
    Find the maximum and minimum of x + 2y + 2z on the sphere x² + y² + z² = 9, by Lagrange multipliers.

Challenge (Problems 46–60)

  • 46.
    Let F be an Archimedean ordered field in which every Cauchy sequence converges. Prove that F has the least upper bound property.
  • 47.
    Prove the Bolzano–Weierstrass theorem in ℝⁿ, and deduce that a continuous real function on a closed bounded subset of ℝⁿ attains its maximum.
  • 48.
    Prove the Cauchy condensation test, and use it to decide the convergence of ∑ₙ₌₃∞ 1/(n ln n (ln ln n)²).
  • 49.
    Prove that lim sup |aₙ|1/n ≤ lim sup |aₙ₊₁/aₙ| for nonzero aₙ, and show with aₙ = 2−n + (−1)ⁿ that the root test can succeed where the ratio test fails.
  • 50.
    Prove the Heine–Cantor theorem, and prove that a continuous f on (0, 1) extends continuously to [0, 1] iff f is uniformly continuous on (0, 1).
  • 51.
    Prove Darboux's theorem: a derivative on an interval has the intermediate value property.
  • 52.
    Let f(x) = e−1/x² for x ≠ 0 and f(0) = 0. Prove f is C∞ on ℝ with f⁽ⁿ⁾(0) = 0 for all n, so f is not analytic at 0.
  • 53.
    Give a proof of ∫₀∞ e−x² dx = √π/2 using only Riemann integrals over squares and discs.
  • 54.
    Prove that a bounded function on [a, b] that is continuous except at finitely many points is Riemann integrable.
  • 55.
    Let d(x) be the distance from x to the nearest integer and T(x) = ∑ₙ₌₀∞ 2⁻ⁿ d(2ⁿx) (the Takagi function). Prove T is continuous, compute T(1/2), T(1/4) and T(1/3), and sketch why T is nowhere differentiable.
  • 56.
    Prove that ∫₀∞ e−x² cos(tx) dx = (√π/2) e−t²/4 for all real t, by differentiating under the integral sign.
  • 57.
    Prove the one-dimensional inverse function theorem: if f is C¹ near a and f′(a) ≠ 0, then f has a C¹ inverse near f(a), with (f⁻¹)′(f(a)) = 1/f′(a).
  • 58.
    Show that the iterated integrals of f(x, y) = (x² − y²)/(x² + y²)² over [0, 1]² are π/4 and −π/4, and prove that ∫∫|f| = ∞.
  • 59.
    Using Vₙ = πⁿ/²/Γ(n/2 + 1) for the volume of the unit n-ball, compute V₅ and V₆, prove that Vₙ → 0, and find the dimension n for which Vₙ is largest.
  • 60.
    Prove ∫₀∞ x/(eˣ − 1) dx = π²/6, using the monotone convergence theorem and the Basel sum ∑ 1/n² = π²/6.
