Problem Set

60 problems

Overview1234567891011121314ProblemsAssessment

These sixty problems are graded in three groups. The Core problems test the computations every course in complex analysis expects. The Advanced problems require combining several results, usually a contour, a residue computation and an estimate. The Challenge problems are proofs or multi-step evaluations of the kind found on qualifying examinations.

Core (Problems 1–20)

  • 1.
    Write (2 + 3i)/(1 − i) in the form a + bi.
  • 2.
    Compute |(3 + 4i)⁵ (1 − i)²|.
  • 3.
    Write −√3 + i in polar form r eiθ with θ = Arg z.
  • 4.
    Compute (−√3 + i)⁶.
  • 5.
    Find all cube roots of i in the form a + bi.
  • 6.
    Find Log(−ei).
  • 7.
    Find the principal value of (1 + i)i in the form a + bi.
  • 8.
    Show that f(z) = Re z is nowhere complex differentiable.
  • 9.
    Verify the Cauchy–Riemann equations for f(z) = ez.
  • 10.
    Find a harmonic conjugate of u = x³ − 3xy².
  • 11.
    Find the radius of convergence of ∑ (n!)² zⁿ/(2n)!.
  • 12.
    Find the radius of convergence of ∑ 2ⁿ z3n.
  • 13.
    Compute ∫ z² dz along any path from 0 to 2i.
  • 14.
    Compute ∮ over |z| = 1 of z̄² dz.
  • 15.
    Compute ∮ over |z| = 2 of dz/(z − 1).
  • 16.
    Compute ∮ over |z| = 1 of cos z/z³ dz.
  • 17.
    Find the Laurent series of z² e1/z about 0 and its residue there.
  • 18.
    Classify the singularity of sin z/z³ at 0.
  • 19.
    Find the residue of 1/(z³ − z) at z = 1.
  • 20.
    Evaluate ∫ dx/(x² + 2x + 2) over ℝ.

Advanced (Problems 21–45)

  • 21.
    Prove that |z − a| < |1 − āz| whenever |z| < 1 and |a| < 1.
  • 22.
    Find all z ∈ ℂ with ez = −2.
  • 23.
    Solve sin z = 2.
  • 24.
    For which real a is u = x³ + a xy² harmonic? For that a, find a holomorphic f with Re f = u.
  • 25.
    Let f be holomorphic on a domain U with |f| constant. Prove that f is constant.
  • 26.
    Prove that ∑ aₙ zⁿ and ∑ n aₙ zn−1 have the same radius of convergence.
  • 27.
    Evaluate ∫₀2π ecos θ cos(sin θ) dθ.
  • 28.
    Compute ∮ over |z| = 3 of ez/(z²(z − 1)) dz.
  • 29.
    Evaluate ∫₀2π dθ/(2 + cos θ).
  • 30.
    Evaluate ∫₀2π cos 3θ/(5 − 4 cos θ) dθ.
  • 31.
    Evaluate ∫ x²/(x⁴ + 1) dx over ℝ.
  • 32.
    Evaluate ∫₀∞ dx/(x⁶ + 1).
  • 33.
    Evaluate ∫ x sin x/(x² + 1) dx over ℝ.
  • 34.
    Evaluate ∫₀∞ ln x/(1 + x²)² dx.
  • 35.
    Evaluate ∫₀∞ x−1/3/(1 + x) dx.
  • 36.
    Evaluate ∫₀∞ sin²x/x² dx.
  • 37.
    Use π cot(πz) to prove that ∑ 1/n⁴ = π⁴/90 (n ≥ 1).
  • 38.
    Evaluate ∑ over n ∈ ℤ of (−1)ⁿ/(n² + 1).
  • 39.
    How many zeros does z⁵ + 15z + 1 have in |z| < 1, and how many in 1 < |z| < 2?
  • 40.
    Let |a| > e. Prove that ez = a zⁿ has exactly n solutions in the unit disc.
  • 41.
    How many zeros does 3z¹⁰⁰ − ez have in the unit disc?
  • 42.
    Find the Möbius transformation T with T(1) = 0, T(i) = 1, T(−1) = ∞, and determine T(𝔻).
  • 43.
    Find the fixed points of T(z) = (2z + 1)/(z + 1) and of S(z) = 1/z, and classify both maps.
  • 44.
    Find the automorphism f of 𝔻 with f(1/2) = 0 and f′(1/2) > 0, and compute f′(1/2).
  • 45.
    Let f : 𝔻 → 𝔻 be holomorphic with f(0) = 1/2. Find the largest possible value of |f′(0)| and a map attaining it.

Challenge (Problems 46–60)

  • 46.
    Let p(z) = zⁿ + aₙ₋₁zn−1 + … + a₀ with M = max |aₖ| > 0. Prove that all zeros of p lie in |z| < 1 + M.
  • 47.
    Prove that an entire function f with Re f(z) ≤ C for all z is constant.
  • 48.
    Let f be entire with |f(z)| ≤ |z|3/2 + 1 for all z. Prove that f(z) = az + b with |b| ≤ 1, and give a nonconstant example.
  • 49.
    Prove that ∫₀∞ cos(x²) dx = ∫₀∞ sin(x²) dx = √(2π)/4.
  • 50.
    For n ≥ 2, prove that ∫₀∞ dx/(1 + xⁿ) = (π/n)/sin(π/n).
  • 51.
    For a > 0, prove that ∑ over n ≥ 1 of 1/(n² + a²) = (πa coth(πa) − 1)/(2a²).
  • 52.
    Prove Γ(s)Γ(1 − s) = π/sin(πs) for 0 < s < 1.
  • 53.
    Prove that f(z) = ∑ zn! has the unit circle as a natural boundary.
  • 54.
    Let fₙ be injective holomorphic functions on a domain U converging uniformly on compact sets to f. Prove that f is injective or constant.
  • 55.
    Prove that ζ(1 + it) ≠ 0 for every real t ≠ 0.
  • 56.
    Prove that ζ(s) = 1/(s − 1) + γ + O(s − 1) as s → 1, where γ is the Euler–Mascheroni constant.
  • 57.
    Prove the Schwarz–Pick inequality |f′(z)| ≤ (1 − |f(z)|²)/(1 − |z|²) for holomorphic f : 𝔻 → 𝔻.
  • 58.
    Show that ℂ and 𝔻 are diffeomorphic as smooth manifolds but not biholomorphic.
  • 59.
    Show that J(z) = (z + 1/z)/2 maps the circle |z| = r > 1 onto an ellipse, and find its semi-axes and foci.
  • 60.
    Let f be holomorphic and injective on a neighbourhood of the closed disc D̄ with boundary C, and let w ∈ f(D) \ f(C). Prove that f⁻¹(w) = (1/2πi) ∮C z f′(z)/(f(z) − w) dz.
