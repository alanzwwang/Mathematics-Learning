🔍 Adding and Subtracting — LCD, Negatives, and Variables

📖 Lesson 4 of 9

👋 Start123456789🏋️ Practice🏆 Quiz

📌 Key idea of this section

You can only add pieces of the SAME size. Match the bottoms first —
then add or subtract the TOPS only. The rule never changes,
even with negative signs and variables on top.

Same bottoms: just count the pieces

2/7 + 3/7 = 5/7

Two sevenths plus three sevenths is five sevenths — like 2 apples + 3 apples. The bottom never gets added: sevenths don't turn into fourteenths when you count them, any more than apples turn into oranges.

Negatives: the number line does the counting

3/4 − 5/4 = −2/4 = −1/2

Three quarters minus five quarters goes two quarters below zero. And with an LCD (least common denominator — the least common multiple of the bottoms; LCM(2, 3) = 6):

−1/2 + 1/3 = −3/6 + 2/6 = −1/6

Start at −3/6, hop 2/6 to the right, land at −1/6. Signs don't change the rule: match the bottoms, combine the tops — the tops are signed numbers now, so "combine" means add with signs.

Variables on top: like terms

x/7 + 3x/7 = 4x/7

One x-seventh plus three x-sevenths is four x-sevenths. Pieces with the same letter part are called like terms, and you combine them by adding their coefficients — the numbers in front (here, 1 and 3).

Different bottoms with variables: the LCD still runs the show

x/2 + x/3 = ?
Step 1: LCD of 2 and 3:   6
Step 2: rewrite each fraction:
        x/2 = 3x/6   (top and bottom × 3)
        x/3 = 2x/6   (top and bottom × 2)
Step 3: combine the tops, keep the bottom:
        3x/6 + 2x/6 = 5x/6   ✅

When x lives on the bottom

What's the LCD of x and 2? Their least common multiple is 2x. Same three steps:

1/x + 1/2 = ?
Step 1: LCD:   2x
Step 2: rewrite:
        1/x = 2/(2x)    (top and bottom × 2)
        1/2 = x/(2x)    (top and bottom × x)
Step 3: combine:
        2/(2x) + x/(2x) = (x + 2)/(2x)   ✅

And one where the bottoms match already — watch it collapse:

2/(3x) + 1/(3x) = 3/(3x) = 1/x   (cancel the shared factor 3)

Subtraction works identically — just subtract the tops, signs and all:

5x/6 − x/4 = ?
LCD of 6 and 4:   12
5x/6 = 10x/12   (top and bottom × 2)
x/4 = 3x/12     (top and bottom × 3)
10x/12 − 3x/12 = 7x/12   ✅

✏️ Your Turn!

  • (a)
    −2/3 + 1/2
  • (b)
    x/4 + x/8
  • (c)
    1/x + 1/3 (LCD: 3x)
👀 Show the answers

Answers: (a) LCD 6: −4/6 + 3/6 = −1/6. (b) LCD 8: 2x/8 + x/8 = 3x/8. (c) 1/x = 3/(3x) and 1/3 = x/(3x), so the sum is (x + 3)/(3x).
