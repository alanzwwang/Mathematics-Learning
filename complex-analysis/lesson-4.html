🔍 Multiplying by i — The Quarter Turn

📖 Lesson 4 of 9

👋 Start123456789🏋️ Practice🏆 Quiz

📌 Key idea of this section

Multiplying by i turns every number
a quarter turn counterclockwise.

The four-step spin cycle

Start at 1 and multiply by i, over and over. Use the one rule, i² = −1, whenever it shows up:

1 → i → i² = −1 → i³ = −i → i⁴ = 1 → i⁵ = i → ...

(Slow motion: i³ = i² × i = −1 × i = −i. And i⁴ = i² × i² = (−1) × (−1) = 1.)

Four multiplications, and you're back home at 1! Now find those answers on the map:

1  is at (1, 0)  — EAST
i  is at (0, 1)  — NORTH
−1 is at (−1, 0) — WEST
−i is at (0, −1) — SOUTH

East, north, west, south, east... Each multiplication by i spins the arrow a QUARTER TURN counterclockwise. That's why i⁴ = 1: four quarter turns make one full turn. The impossible number is a spinning machine!

Riding the cycle

The answers repeat every four: 1, i, −1, −i, 1, i, −1, −i, ... So you can simplify ANY power of i by counting how many complete spins to throw away — remainders, just like the Number Theory lesson!

i¹⁰ = ?   10 = 4 × 2 + 2,  so throw away 2 full turns:
i¹⁰ = i² = −1

Everything spins, not just 1

The quarter turn works on every number:

i · 3 = 3i              (3, 0) → (0, 3)      east to north ✅
i · 2i = −2             (0, 2) → (−2, 0)     north to west ✅
i · (1 + i) = −1 + i    (1, 1) → (−1, 1)     diagonal, spun ✅

(Sharp eyes: a quarter turn sends the point (x, y) to (−y, x). Check it on all three!)

And the most useful spin fact of all: TWO quarter turns = a half turn = multiplying by −1. Of course — that's just i² = −1 said in spinning language. The one rule was a rotation rule all along.
