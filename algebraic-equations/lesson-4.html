🔍 Multi-Step Equations — Unwrap in Reverse Order

📖 Lesson 4 of 12

👋 Start123456789101112🏋️ Practice🏆 Quiz

📌 KEY IDEA

An equation like 3x − 7 = 11 dresses x in layers. Peel the layers off in REVERSE order: undo the adding or subtracting first, then the multiplying or dividing.

Socks and Shoes

Here is the trick, and it is pure common sense. In the morning you put on your socks first, then your shoes. At night, which comes off first? The shoes — the last thing that went on. You undo everything in reverse order.

Same with equations. In 3x − 7, x got dressed like this:

1. First layer:  × 3
2. Second layer (on top):  − 7

So to undress x, go backwards: undo the −7 first, then the ×3.

Worked Example 1: 3x − 7 = 11

3x − 7 + 7 = 11 + 7       add 7 to both sides (peel off the top layer)
3x = 18                   −7 + 7 cancels; 11 + 7 = 18
3x ÷ 3 = 18 ÷ 3           divide both sides by 3 (peel off the bottom layer)
x = 6                     3 ÷ 3 = 1; 18 ÷ 3 = 6

Check: 3 × 6 − 7 = 18 − 7 = 11 ✅

Worked Example 2: (2/3)x + 5 = 11

Fractions follow the same socks-and-shoes rule. The +5 is on top, so it comes off first:

(2/3)x + 5 − 5 = 11 − 5   subtract 5 from both sides
(2/3)x = 6                +5 − 5 cancels; 11 − 5 = 6
(3/2) × (2/3)x = 6 × (3/2)   multiply both sides by the reciprocal 3/2
x = 9                     (3/2)(2/3) = 1; 6 × 3/2 = 18/2 = 9

Check: (2/3) × 9 + 5 = 6 + 5 = 11 ✅

Worked Example 3: (x − 4)/3 = 2

The whole package (x − 4) was divided by 3. Undo that outer layer first:

3 × (x − 4)/3 = 3 × 2     multiply both sides by 3
x − 4 = 6                 the 3's cancel; 3 × 2 = 6
x − 4 + 4 = 6 + 4         add 4 to both sides
x = 10                    −4 + 4 cancels; 6 + 4 = 10

Check: (10 − 4)/3 = 6/3 = 2 ✅

Worked Example 4: 8 − 2x = 2 (two methods!)

This one has a trap: x is being multiplied by −2. Here are TWO correct ways through.

Method A — subtract 8 first:

8 − 2x − 8 = 2 − 8        subtract 8 from both sides
−2x = −6                  8 − 8 cancels; 2 − 8 = −6
−2x ÷ (−2) = −6 ÷ (−2)    divide both sides by −2
x = 3                     negative ÷ negative = positive

Method B — add 2x first (moves x to the other side with a happy positive sign):

8 − 2x + 2x = 2 + 2x      add 2x to both sides
8 = 2 + 2x                −2x + 2x cancels
8 − 2 = 2 + 2x − 2        subtract 2 from both sides
6 = 2x                    8 − 2 = 6; 2 − 2 cancels
3 = x                     divide both sides by 2

Same answer, x = 3, both times! When two legal paths lead to the same place, pick whichever feels safer to you.

Check: 8 − 2 × 3 = 8 − 6 = 2 ✅

Why the Order Matters

What if you divided by 3 FIRST in 3x − 7 = 11? You would get x − 7/3 = 11/3 — fractions everywhere, yuck! The socks-and-shoes rule keeps the numbers friendly: remove the added or subtracted part first, then deal with the multiplication or division.

✏️ Your Turn!

Solve (x + 6)/5 = 2. (What is the outer layer — what was done LAST to x?)

👀 Show the answers

Answer: Multiply both sides by 5: x + 6 = 10. Then subtract 6: x = 4. Check: (4 + 6)/5 = 10/5 = 2 ✅
