📌 EVERYTHING, ONE LAST TIME

Same thing to both sides. Unwrap in reverse order. Respect signs and mirrors. Always check.

The Recap

Situation                          Your move
x + (−9) = 4                       add 9 to both sides             → x = 13
x − (−6) = 10                      rewrite as x + 6, subtract 6    → x = 4
−5x = 45                           divide by −5                    → x = −9
(3/4)x = 12                        multiply by the reciprocal 4/3  → x = 16
3x − 7 = 11                        reverse order: +7, then ÷3       → x = 6
4(x − 3) = 20                      divide first OR distribute       → x = 8
12 − 2(x − 3) = 6                  distribute the −2 with care      → x = 6
7x − 4 = 3x + 12                   subtract 3x, then unwrap         → x = 4
2(x + 3) = 2x + 6                  x's vanish, leftover true        → infinitely many
2(x + 3) = 2x + 5                  x's vanish, leftover false       → no solution
3x² = 48                           unwrap, then remember ±          → x = ±4
x² = 5x                            factor, never divide by x        → x = 0 or 5
f(x) = 3x − 2, f(x) = 16           run the machine backwards        → x = 6
−3x < 12                           divide by −3 and FLIP            → x > −4
x + y = 10, x − y = 2              eliminate or substitute          → (6, 4)

The Magic Sentence (Honors Upgrade)

Whatever you do to one side, do to the other — unwrap in reverse order,
watch every sign, and check.

Say it out loud three times. Seriously! That sentence now covers one-step equations, systems, inequalities, and your first quadratics all at once.

Why This Matters

Every science formula, every phone plan, every rocket trajectory, every video game calculating your score — underneath them all sit little equations being solved by the Golden Rule. And everything coming next in math — the quadratic formula, graphing lines and parabolas, physics, exponential growth — is built from the exact moves in this file.

But the biggest lesson isn't the moves. It's this:

An equation is a balance. Keep it balanced, and no variable can hide from you.
