📌 KEY IDEA

A solution is a number you can plug in for the variable that makes the equation true. Verifying means plugging it back in to check — and professionals always check.

From Boxes to Letters

Long ago, students wrote problems with empty boxes:

□ + 5 = 12

"What number goes in the box?" Eventually mathematicians got tired of drawing boxes and started using letters. There is nothing special or scary about x: it is a number wearing a mask. Our job is to unmask it.

What Is a Solution?

A solution is a number you can plug in for x that makes the equation true — that makes the scale balance. Let's verify one, honors style, with a negative answer:

Is x = −3 a solution of 2x + 11 = 5?

Left side:  2 × (−3) + 11 = −6 + 11 = 5
Right side: 5
5 = 5  ✅

Balanced! So x = −3 IS the solution. Notice the negative number plugged in calmly and correctly. That is normal from now on.

Solutions can also be fractions. Is x = 3/2 a solution of 4x − 2 = 4?

Left side:  4 × (3/2) − 2 = 6 − 2 = 4
Right side: 4
4 = 4  ✅

Yes! If you were guessing whole numbers only, you would have searched forever. This is exactly why we need a method instead of guessing.

Verifying with x on Both Sides

When x appears on both sides, plug your answer into EACH side separately and compare:

Verify x = 9 for 5x − 7 = 3x + 11:

Left side:  5 × 9 − 7 = 45 − 7 = 38
Right side: 3 × 9 + 11 = 27 + 11 = 38
38 = 38  ✅

Both sides give the same number, so x = 9 checks out. That check is called verifying the solution, and you'll do it for every equation you ever solve. It is free and takes fifteen seconds!

The Problem with Guessing

For x + 5 = 12, guessing works fine. But what about these?

(3/4)x − 5 = 7
2(x − 3) = 5x + 9
x² = 5x

Guessing could take all afternoon — and some of these answers are negative or fractions, or there is more than one answer! We need a machine that finds x every time, with zero luck involved. That machine has one rule, and it is in the next lesson.

✏️ Your Turn!

Is x = −5 a solution of 20 − 2x = 30? Plug it in and check.

👀 Show the answers

Answer: Yes! Left side: 20 − 2 × (−5) = 20 − (−10) = 20 + 10 = 30. Right side: 30. ✅
