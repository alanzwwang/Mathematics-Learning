⚡ The Distributive Property, Like Terms, and Factoring

📌 KEY IDEA

a(b + c) = ab + ac. Multiplication distributes over addition — and it works backwards too, which is called factoring.

What "Distribute" Means

To distribute means to hand something out to everyone inside the parentheses. Watch with plain numbers first:

3(4 + 5) = 3 × 9 = 27           one way: add inside first
3(4 + 5) = 3×4 + 3×5 = 12 + 15 = 27   other way: distribute the 3

Same answer — that is the distributive property: a(b + c) = ab + ac. It also works with subtraction: a(b − c) = ab − ac. And it works with negatives, which is where honors students earn their stripes:

−2(x − 4) = −2 × x + (−2) × (−4) = −2x + 8

Did you see it? −2 times −4 is +8. The minus sign gets distributed too! This is the single most common place students lose points, so stare at it until it feels obvious.

Worked Example 1: 4(x − 3) = 20 (two methods again!)

Method A — divide first (works because 20 divides evenly by 4):

4(x − 3) ÷ 4 = 20 ÷ 4     divide both sides by 4
x − 3 = 5                 the 4's cancel; 20 ÷ 4 = 5
x = 8                     add 3 to both sides

Method B — distribute first:

4x − 12 = 20              distribute: 4 × x = 4x and 4 × (−3) = −12
4x = 32                   add 12 to both sides
x = 8                     divide both sides by 4

Same answer! Method A is faster when the division comes out even; Method B always works, even when it doesn't.

Check: 4 × (8 − 3) = 4 × 5 = 20 ✅

Worked Example 2: 3(x + 2) − 5 = 16

3x + 6 − 5 = 16           distribute the 3: 3 × x and 3 × 2
3x + 1 = 16               combine the plain numbers: 6 − 5 = 1
3x = 15                   subtract 1 from both sides
x = 5                     divide both sides by 3

Check: 3 × (5 + 2) − 5 = 21 − 5 = 16 ✅

Worked Example 3: 12 − 2(x − 3) = 6 (the sign trap!)

12 − 2x + 6 = 6           distribute −2: −2 × x = −2x, and −2 × (−3) = +6
18 − 2x = 6               combine the plain numbers: 12 + 6 = 18
−2x = −12                 subtract 18 from both sides
x = 6                     divide both sides by −2

Check: 12 − 2 × (6 − 3) = 12 − 6 = 6 ✅

Combining Like Terms

Like terms are terms with the exact same letter part: 7x and −2x are like terms; 7x and 7y are not. You combine like terms by adding their coefficients:

7x − 2x = (7 − 2)x = 5x

Why does that work? It is the distributive property running backwards! Seven x's minus two x's leaves five x's, the same way seven apples minus two apples leaves five apples.

Worked Example 4: 7x − 3 − 2x = 17

5x − 3 = 17               combine like terms: 7x − 2x = 5x
5x = 20                   add 3 to both sides
x = 4                     divide both sides by 5

Check: 7 × 4 − 3 − 2 × 4 = 28 − 3 − 8 = 17 ✅

Factoring — Distribution in Reverse

Factoring means pulling a common piece OUT of every term and writing it in front of parentheses:

6x + 15 = 3(2x + 5)

Why is that legal? Because 3 × 2x = 6x and 3 × 5 = 15 — distribute the 3 and you get right back to where you started. Factoring and distributing are the same road in opposite directions. Always check factoring by re-distributing!

Verify with x = 3: left side: 6 × 3 + 15 = 33. right side: 3 × (2 × 3 + 5) = 3 × 11 = 33. ✅

Factoring feels like a trick now, but it becomes a superpower in Lesson 7, when it cracks open quadratic equations.
