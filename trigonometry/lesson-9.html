📌 Everything, one last time

Opposite = across · Adjacent = next to (not the hypotenuse) · Hypotenuse = longest side
SOH CAH TOA:  sin = O/H · cos = A/H · tan = O/A
Pythagorean theorem: leg² + leg² = hypotenuse²
Special angles: sin 30° = cos 60° = 0.5 · tan 45° = 1 · sin 60° = cos 30° ≈ 0.87

The recap list

  • ·
    A right triangle has one 90° angle; the hypotenuse sits across from it and is always the longest side.
  • ·
    Opposite and adjacent depend on which angle you pick — switch angles and they swap. The hypotenuse never changes.
  • ·
    Same angle, same ratio — no matter the triangle's size. 3/5 = 6/10 = 9/15.
  • ·
    sin = opposite ÷ hypotenuse, cos = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse, tan = opposite ÷ adjacent. SOH CAH TOA!
  • ·
    sin of an angle equals cos of its partner — partners add to 90°.
  • ·
    sin and cos are always less than 1 (the hypotenuse is on the bottom). tan can be bigger than 1.
  • ·
    Pythagorean theorem: leg² + leg² = hypotenuse². Add to find a hypotenuse; subtract to find a leg.
  • ·
    The special angles: 30° (sin = 0.5), 45° (tan = 1), 60° (cos = 0.5).
  • ·
    Measuring the world: height = sin × slant, reach = cos × slant, height = tan × shadow.

The magic sentence

Pick your angle.
Name your sides.
SOH CAH TOA.
Same angle, same ratio — forever.

Say it out loud three times. Seriously! That's the whole lesson in four lines.

Why this matters

Trigonometry is the math of measuring the unreachable. Every ramp you roll down, every roof over your head, every GPS satellite overhead, every animated character jumping across a screen — they all lean on the three fractions you just learned. And notice how one humble idea from Lesson 3 — same angle, same ratio — quietly unlocked everything else. Math ideas are never alone — they hold hands.
