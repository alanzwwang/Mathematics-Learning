🔍 Sine, Cosine, Tangent — SOH CAH TOA

📖 Lesson 4 of 9

👋 Start123456789🏋️ Practice✅ Answers

📌 Key idea of this section

sin = Opposite ÷ Hypotenuse   (SOH)
cos = Adjacent ÷ Hypotenuse   (CAH)
tan = Opposite ÷ Adjacent     (TOA)

Three ratios showed up in Lesson 3 — and they turn out to be so useful that mathematicians gave them names. They're just nicknames for fractions of sides:

· SINE (say "sign"), written sin: opposite ÷ hypotenuse

· COSINE (say "CO-sign"), written cos: adjacent ÷ hypotenuse

· TANGENT (say "TAN-jent"), written tan: opposite ÷ adjacent

How do you keep them straight? Chant the most famous made-up word in math class:

SOH · CAH · TOA     (say "so-kuh-TOE-uh")

S-OH: Sine is Opposite over Hypotenuse. C-AH: Cosine is Adjacent over Hypotenuse. T-OA: Tangent is Opposite over Adjacent. Chant it five times right now. By problem 17, you'll say it in your sleep.

Let's compute all three for a 3-4-5 triangle. Angle A faces side 3, so for angle A: opposite = 3, adjacent = 4, hypotenuse = 5.

sin A = 3/5       cos A = 4/5       tan A = 3/4

Now switch to angle B, which faces side 4. The labels swap: opposite = 4, adjacent = 3.

sin B = 4/5       cos B = 3/5       tan B = 4/3

Bonus discovery — look closely at those two rows: sin A = 3/5 and cos B = 3/5. They're EQUAL! That's not luck. The side opposite angle A is adjacent to angle B, so sine of one pointy angle always equals cosine of the other. And since every right triangle's two pointy angles add to 90° (they share the 90° left over from the right angle), the rule is: sin of an angle = cos of its partner, where partners add to 90°.

One last pattern worth noticing: sin and cos both have the hypotenuse — the LONGEST side — on the bottom. So sin and cos are always smaller than 1. But tan divides leg by leg, and either leg can be longer — so tan can be bigger than 1 (like 4/3 up there!). Remember that; the practice problems will test it.

✏️ Your Turn!

A right triangle has legs 5 and 12, and hypotenuse 13. Angle C faces side 5. Compute sin C, cos C, and tan C.

👀 Show the answers

Answers: opposite = 5, adjacent = 12, hypotenuse = 13. So sin C = 5/13, cos C = 12/13, tan C = 5/12.
