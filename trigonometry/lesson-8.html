⚠️ Watch Out! Common Mistakes

📖 Lesson 8 of 9

👋 Start123456789🏋️ Practice🏆 Quiz

📌 Keep the big ideas in sight

Pick your angle FIRST. The labels move when the angle moves.
sin and cos are always less than 1. tan can be anything.

These five mistakes catch students every single year. Learn them now, and they won't catch you!

Mistake 1: Staring at the wrong angle

In a 3-4-5 triangle, sin = 3/5.   ❌

For WHICH angle? Sin of the angle facing side 3 is 3/5 — but sin of the angle facing side 4 is 4/5! "Opposite" and "adjacent" are meaningless until you pick your angle. Circle the angle in your sketch first, every time. ✅

Mistake 2: Mixing up sine and cosine

Angle A faces side 3 in a 3-4-5 triangle, so cos A = 3/5?   ❌

That's SINE. SOH versus CAH: sine takes the Opposite, cosine takes the Adjacent. cos A = 4/5. When in doubt, chant it out: SOH — CAH — TOA. ✅

Mistake 3: Giving tangent a hypotenuse

In a 5-12-13 triangle, tan of the angle facing side 5 is 5/13?   ❌

TOA: tangent is Opposite ÷ Adjacent — the two LEGS. The hypotenuse never appears in a tangent. The correct answer is 5/12. If you see a 13 under a tan, an alarm should ring. ✅

Mistake 4: Thinking a bigger triangle has bigger trig values

The 6-8-10 triangle is double the 3-4-5, so its sine is double?   ❌

6/10 simplifies to 3/5! Same angles, same ratios — the size of the triangle is irrelevant. That was the whole point of Lesson 3, and it's what makes trig powerful. ✅

Mistake 5: Swapping the special-angle twins

sin 60° = 0.5?   ❌

That's sin 30° (and cos 60°). Sine GROWS as the angle grows, so sin 60° must be the bigger value: ≈ 0.87. Before you answer, ask yourself: should this number be the small one (0.5) or the big one (0.87)? ✅
