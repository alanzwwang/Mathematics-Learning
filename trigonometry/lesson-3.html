🎯 The Big Idea — Same Angle, Same Ratio

📖 Lesson 3 of 9

👋 Start123456789🏋️ Practice🏆 Quiz

📌 Key idea of this section

A ratio is a fraction made from two sides.
Same angle → same ratio, no matter how big the triangle is.
3/5 = 6/10 = 9/15

Here comes the idea that ALL of trigonometry is built on. Don't blink!

A RATIO is just a fraction comparing two numbers — like opposite side ÷ hypotenuse. Now watch what happens when we compute that ratio in three different triangles. Each one has an angle that faces the first side listed:

Triangle        opposite ÷ hypotenuse
3-4-5           3/5
6-8-10          6/10 = 3/5
9-12-15         9/15 = 3/5

Three different-sized triangles — one identical ratio! That's no accident. The 6-8-10 triangle is just the 3-4-5 with every side doubled, and the 9-12-15 is the 3-4-5 tripled. Doubling the top AND bottom of a fraction never changes its value.

So here's the big idea: the ratio depends ONLY on the angle — never on the size of the triangle. A tiny triangle and a giant triangle with the same angles give the exact same ratios. The ratio is the angle's FINGERPRINT. Two triangles might be different sizes, but if they share an angle, that angle's ratios are identical.

This is why trig works at all! A mathematician can measure a tiny paper triangle, and you can use those same ratios on a triangle the size of a mountain. Same angle, same ratio — forever and ever.

✏️ Your Turn!

A right triangle has legs 9 and 12, and hypotenuse 15. (a) Compute opposite ÷ hypotenuse for the angle facing side 9, and simplify. (b) Which famous triangle has the same angles as this one?

👀 Show the answers

Answers: (a) 9/15 = 3/5. (b) The 3-4-5 triangle! The 9-12-15 is the 3-4-5 tripled — so its angles (and its ratios) match exactly.
